03 • Engineering Design & Prototype

VEXO Vertical Fixed-Bed Catalytic Reactor

Modular cylindrical reactor engineered for low pressure drop, uniform airflow, and real-time sensor validation.

Exploded & Assembled View of VEXO Catalytic Reactor

🔬 Whole-Matrix Synergistic Principle

Retains Qena sludge's native SiO₂ (48.14%) & Al₂O₃ (21.41%) as a porous mechanical support while Fe₂O₃ (10.61%) & MnO₂ (1.01%) catalyze O₃ breakdown.

⚗️ Surface Redox Reaction Mechanism

Step 1: O₃ + Mn³⁺/Fe²⁺ → O₂ + O⁻(ads) + Mn⁴⁺/Fe³⁺
Step 2: O₃ + O⁻(ads) → 2O₂ + e⁻ (Site Regeneration)

🌬️ Airflow & Sensor Architecture

Bottom DC fan draws polluted air through a 2–4 mm granulated sludge bed held by stainless meshes, monitored by dual C_in and C_out ozone sensors.

04 • Processing & Validation

4-Step Engineering Workflow

01. Pre-Washing

Removes soluble salts & organic impurities to expose active Fe/Mn sites.

02. Granulation

Forms 2–4 mm pellets to prevent airflow blockage (ΔP < 20 Pa).

03. O₃ Catalysis

Converts toxic O₃ directly into breathable O₂ at room temperature.

04. Regeneration

Fe–Mn synergy resists humidity and restores activity via mild heating.
