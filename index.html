03 • Our Engineering Solution

VEXO: Whole-Matrix Solution, Prototype & Mechanism

Preserving the native multicomponent matrix of Qena groundwater sludge to drive low-cost catalytic ozone breakdown.

Exploded & Assembled View: VEXO Vertical Fixed-Bed Catalytic Reactor with C_in / C_out Sensors.

🔬 Core Innovation: Whole-Matrix Synergy

Instead of isolating manganese alone, VEXO keeps native SiO₂ (48.14%) and Al₂O₃ (21.41%) as a high-strength porous support while Fe₂O₃ (10.61%) and MnO₂ (1.01%) form moisture-resistant redox pairs.

⚙️ Catalytic Reaction Mechanism

1) O₃ + Mn³⁺/Fe²⁺ → O₂ + O⁻(ads) + Mn⁴⁺/Fe³⁺
2) O₃ + O⁻(ads) → 2O₂ + e⁻ (Active Site Regeneration)

📐 Engineering Specifications

2–4 mm washed sludge granules sandwiched between stainless steel meshes to minimize pressure drop (ΔP < 20 Pa) at controlled fan airflow.

📈 XRD Crystalline Phases & Target Validation

  • Amorphous/Poorly Crystalline Fe–Mn Oxides: Broad XRD peaks indicate high surface defect density (oxygen vacancies), ideal for rapid ozone adsorption.
  • Quartz & Aluminosilicate Peaks: Confirm rigid thermal framework capable of withstanding 300°C regeneration.
  • Next Stage Validation: Dynamic fixed-bed testing targeting >85% O₃ removal across 30%–80% relative humidity.
