What is VEXO?

From water-treatment waste to a potential air-pollution control material.

THE WASTE

Groundwater-treatment sludge generated as a waste stream in Qena.

THE PROBLEM

Ground-level ozone is a harmful air pollutant that requires effective removal.

THE APPROACH

Use the whole sludge matrix as a functional filter and catalytic material.

THE GOAL

Develop a lower-cost and locally sourced alternative for ozone-removal systems.
