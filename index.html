VEXO
ISEF Research Project • Qena, Egypt

Engineering Waste.
Rethinking Clean Air.

Transforming native whole groundwater-treatment sludge from Qena into a cost-effective, multifunctional catalytic material for gas-phase ozone (O₃) removal.

Core Approach
Whole-Matrix Retention
Target Pollutant
Ground-Level Ozone (O₃)
Feedstock Source
Qena Water Residuals
Engineering Target
>85% O₃ Removal
01 — Novelty & Methodology

Keep the matrix. Don't isolate one component.

Instead of energy-intensive chemical extraction, VEXO utilizes the natural synergy of iron, manganese, and aluminosilicates present in Qena sludge.
