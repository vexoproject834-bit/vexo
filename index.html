01 — Novelty & Methodology

Keep the matrix. Don't isolate one component.

Instead of energy-intensive chemical extraction, VEXO utilizes the natural synergy of iron, manganese, and aluminosilicates present in Qena sludge.

CONVENTIONAL PATHWAY • HIGH COST

Single-Component Extraction

  • ✕ Chemically separate one target metal or mineral.
  • ✕ High energy use & secondary chemical waste.
  • ✕ Requires expensive synthetic dopants (e.g., CeO₂).
VEXO INNOVATION • CIRCULAR ECONOMY

Whole-Matrix Valorization

  • ✓ Retain native Qena sludge matrix intact.
  • ✓ Simple physical washing & granulation engineering.
  • ✓ Synergistic Fe₂O₃–MnO₂–SiO₂ active interfaces.
