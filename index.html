⚙ Whole-Matrix Catalytic Mechanism

Four sequential engineering steps transforming Qena water-plant waste into a high-efficiency ozone filter.

01

Pre-Washing & Drying

Removes soluble salts & organic impurities to expose active Fe/Mn catalytic sites.

02

Granulation (2–4 mm)

Forms uniform pellets supported by SiO₂ (48.1%) & Al₂O₃ (21.4%) to prevent air pressure drop.

03

Redox O₃ Decomposition

Mn⁴⁺/Mn³⁺ & Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ electron transfer converts toxic O₃ directly into harmless O₂.

04

Humidity Resistance

Fe–Mn synergy prevents water vapor poisoning and enables thermal regeneration.
